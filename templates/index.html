HOW AQUAWISE DECIDES

Understand your irrigation recommendation

These are prototype rules using model-estimated soil moisture and forecast rainfall. They are not universal crop irrigation thresholds.

💧

Irrigate — verify first

Low estimated moisture (below 15%) and less than 5 mm forecast rain. Check root-zone moisture and crop needs before watering.

⌕

Check the field

Moisture estimate unavailable or between 15% and 30%. Inspect the root zone, crop condition, and schedule before deciding.

🌦

Wait — rain is forecast

At least 5 mm rain is forecast in the next 24 hours. Wait and reassess the field.
