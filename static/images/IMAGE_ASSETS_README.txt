AquaWise image assets
=====================
The app now uses locally bundled photographic tiles for:
- Soil selection: red, black, sandy, clay and loamy soil
- Crop categories: grains, pulses, vegetables, fruits and cash crops

The photo tiles are included locally, so they do not require Google image loading or an internet connection.
The scenic farm background is farm-background.png.
The soil/crop collage was generated for this project and split into separate image files.
Older SVG illustrations are retained as fallback/reference assets but are no longer used for the soil and category cards.
